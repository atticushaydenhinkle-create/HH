COMPOUND MITER (sloped sides / legs)

Example
4-sided planter, sides sloped 30°
  1. Each side leans out 30° from straight up (plumb). Enter Tilt 30.
  2. It's a box, so 4 sides. Enter Sides 4.
  3. Bandsaw: set the vise to 45° (180 ÷ 4 sides). Stand the piece against the vise jaw and lean it 30° from vertical — the same way it sits in the planter — shim it and check with an angle finder on the face. Cut.
  4. Miter saw: lay the piece flat, set the miter to 26.6° and tilt the blade bevel to 37.8°.
  5. Cut both ends of all 4 sides, flipping the piece so the cuts mirror. Dry-fit before tacking.
Answers: bandsaw vise 45° with the part leaned 30° (same cut as miter 26.57° + bevel 37.76° on a tilting saw, bevel measured as blade tilt from 0°). Do not set the vise to 26.57° and roll the part 37.76° — that gives a different, wrong cut.

ROLLED / ARC

Example
Roll 2" square tube to a 48" radius, 90° arc
  1. Radius is to the centerline of the tube: 48".
  2. Centerline arc = 48 × π/2 = 75.40". Cut stock to that plus straight tails for the roller to grab.
  3. Inside face (R 47) = 73.83"; outside face (R 49) = 76.97".
  4. Check: chord (end to end, straight) should read 67.88" (≈67 7/8") and rise at the middle 14.06" (≈14 1/16") on the centerline.
  5. Load the example: the calculator gives back Radius 48, angle 90°, arc 75.40.
